# Wallet & POS Payment Platform

A hybrid digital wallet and point-of-sale (POS) payment platform designed to support secure, convenient, and cashless transactions.

The platform connects a mobile wallet application with merchant POS terminals through a centralized backend. It supports wallet operations, QR-based payments, merchant transactions, and integration with external payment providers.

> **Project status:** Under active development.

## Overview

The platform consists of two main applications connected through a backend API:

- **Mobile Application** — Allows users to manage their wallets, view transactions, top up their balances, and authorize POS payments using QR codes.
- **Backend API** — Handles authentication, wallet operations, payment processing, transaction management, and external payment provider integrations.
- **POS Integration** — Enables merchants to initiate payments by scanning QR codes generated by the mobile application.

## Key Features

- Digital wallet and balance management
- QR-based POS payment authorization
- Offline QR generation with online payment authorization
- Double-entry ledger for financial transactions
- Merchant and POS terminal management
- Top-ups and withdrawals through external payment providers
- Transaction history and payment reconciliation
- Secure communication and transaction validation
